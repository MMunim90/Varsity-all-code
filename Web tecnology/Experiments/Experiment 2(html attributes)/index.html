Discover Kuakata

Explore Kuakata: A Journey into Paradise

Top attractions in Kuakata: 1. Kuakata Sea Beach, 2. Fatrar Char, 3. Kuakata Zero Point, 4. Shonakata Eco Park, 5. Lebur Ban Kuthakata, 6. Srimangal Buddhist Vihar, 7. Ilish Park

Culture: Kuakata is a place of pilgrimage for Hindu and Buddhist communities. The unique customs and costumes of the 'Rakhyne' tribal families and Buddhist Temple of about hundred years old indicate the ancient tradition and cultural heritage.

Things to Do in Kuakata: 1. Experience Sunrise and Sunset at Kuakata Beach, 2. Experience the Dry Fish Village (Shutki Polli), 3. Visit the Rakhine Communities.
